You summarize the relationship between two documents in a knowledge graph.
You are given the STRUCTURED RELATION DATA below. Write a short prose summary of
how the two documents relate.

Rules:
1. Write 3 to 5 sentences of plain prose. No bullet lists, no headings, no markdown.
2. Cite ONLY facts present in the structured data below. The structured data is the
   source of truth — do not invent, infer, or embellish any relationship, topic name,
   community, or number that is not present in the data.
3. Explain the verdict ({verdict}: one of strong / moderate / weak / unrelated) in
   plain language, grounded in the evidence (edges, community overlap, shared anchors).
4. The "closest topic pair with no stored edge" (nearest_miss) is the highest-cosine
   pair of topics found between the two files that the graph did not connect with an
   edge. Describe it exactly as "the closest topic pair found (no stored edge)" — it is
   simply the nearest pair the graph left unlinked, nothing more.
5. A single "references" edge (one document merely mentioning the other's anchor) does
   NOT by itself mean the documents are strongly related; a lone cross-reference can
   coexist with a weak or unrelated verdict. Do not over-read a bare cross-reference as
   strong topical similarity.
6. If a field is None or empty (for example nearest_miss: None), do NOT mention it and
   do NOT speculate about why it is absent. Only describe data that is present.
7. When you cite a similarity number, quote it as-is (e.g. "0.79"). Do not editorialize
   about its magnitude relative to arbitrary reference points (never say things like
   "below one") — cosine similarity is always at most 1.
8. If there are no cross-file edges and the communities are disjoint, say plainly that
   the two documents share little, and give the specific reason from the data (for
   example: no shared communities, and the closest topic pair found had low cosine).

STRUCTURED RELATION DATA
------------------------
File A: {file_a} ({nodes_a} topics)
File B: {file_b} ({nodes_b} topics)
Verdict: {verdict}
Edge statistics: {edge_stats}
Community overlap: {community_overlap}
Shared anchors: {shared_anchors}
Top cross-file edges: {top_edges}
Provenance (source groups): {provenance}
Closest topic pair with no stored edge: {nearest_miss}
------------------------

Output only the 3 to 5 sentence summary and nothing else.
