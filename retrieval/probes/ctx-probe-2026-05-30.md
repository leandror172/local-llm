## Decisions

| Model | Old num_ctx | New num_ctx | Rationale |
|-------|------------|------------|-----------|
| qwen3:14b | 16384 | **32768** | 1,051 MiB headroom at 32K; 16.5 tok/s acceptable |
| qwen2.5-coder:14b | 16384 | **32768** | 2,790 MiB headroom at 32K; 14.9 tok/s acceptable |
